## 🎯 Session Flow At-A-Glance

### Minutes 0-2: Hook & Introduction
**Your Opening:** "Imagine analyzing 500,000 survey responses as easily as 500... for free... and never losing your work."

**Key Message:** Pandas makes faculty research easier, faster, and more reproducible.

### Minutes 2-30: Teaching & Demonstration
**Notebook:** `01_Teaching_Demonstration_Notebook.ipynb`

**What You'll Do:**
- Run cells live as you explain concepts
- Encourage faculty to follow along (optional)
- Pause after each module for quick check-in
- Use chat polls: "Who's with me so far?"

**Module Breakdown:**
- Module 1 (9 min): Setup, Pandas basics, Essential Four commands
- Module 2 (8 min): Data exploration, missing values, research questions
- Module 3 (7 min): Filtering, creating variables, handling missing data
- Module 4 (6 min): Visualizations with Pandas and Seaborn

**Pro Tips:**
- Keep energy HIGH - this fights tech anxiety
- Acknowledge frustration: "Yes, syntax is weird at first!"
- Show mistakes: "Oops, forgot a parenthesis - this happens to everyone!"
- Use humor: "If I can do this, anyone can!"

### Minute 30: Transition to Practice
**Your Script:**
*"Okay, you've seen me do it. Now it's YOUR turn. I'm sharing the practice notebook link in chat RIGHT NOW. You have 15 minutes to work through the exercises. Don't worry about finishing - just start with Exercise 1 and go as far as you can. I'll be monitoring chat for questions!"*

**Action Items:**
1. Post practice notebook link in chat
2. Give 30 seconds for everyone to open it
3. Verbally confirm: "Everyone has it open?"
4. Set timer for 15 minutes
5. Monitor chat actively

### Minutes 30-45: Practice Time
**Your Role:**
- Stay on video/screen share
- Monitor Zoom chat closely
- Answer questions immediately
- Offer encouragement: "Great progress!"
- At minute 40, give 5-minute warning

**If Someone's Stuck:**
- Screen share their notebook (with permission)
- Walk through solution together
- This helps EVERYONE learning

### Minutes 45-60: Solutions & Wrap-Up
**Notebook:** `03_Solutions_with_Explanations.ipynb`

**Your Script:**
*"Time's up! Don't worry if you didn't finish - that's totally normal. Let's go through solutions together. I'll show you multiple ways to solve each problem."*

**What to Show:**
- Walk through Exercise 1 completely
- Highlight 2-3 solutions from Exercise 2
- Show 1-2 visualizations from Exercise 4
- Don't go through EVERY solution - time limited!

**Minutes 55-60: Closing**
- Recap "Faculty Five" takeaways
- Share resources document
- Announce office hours
- Call to action: "Load YOUR data by Friday!"
- Thank participants

## 💡 Teaching Tips from the Field

### Managing Pacing

**If Running Ahead (Rare):**
- Add bonus Seaborn examples
- Show advanced filtering (.query() method)
- Demo reading from Google Sheets
- Q&A session

**If Running Behind (Common):**
- Skip detailed Seaborn section (show just one example)
- Reduce practice time to 12 minutes
- Show only 3-4 solutions (not all)
- Send full solutions notebook via email

### Handling Tech Issues

**"I can't see the notebook!"**
- Check sharing permissions
- Re-share link in chat
- Offer to email directly

**"I'm getting an error!"**
- "Great! Errors are learning opportunities!"
- Ask them to paste error in chat
- Often it's a simple typo
- Fix together on screen

**"Nothing is working!"**
- Stay calm and encouraging
- Switch them to "follow-along" mode
- They can try again after session
- Offer 1-on-1 office hours

### Reading the Room

**Signs People Are Lost:**
- Chat goes silent
- No one asking questions
- Blank stares on video

**Solution:**
- Stop and check in: "Everyone with me?"
- Quick poll: "Type 1 if you're following"
- Repeat last concept
- Slow down

**Signs People Are Engaged:**
- Questions in chat
- "Aha!" reactions
- People sharing screens
- Typing visible

**Solution:**
- Keep momentum going
- Call on people: "Sarah, what surprised you?"
- Celebrate discoveries
