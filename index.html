❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need programming experience?
Not at all! This workshop is designed for faculty with zero coding background. We start from the very beginning.
Do I need to install anything?
No! We use Google Colab, which runs entirely in your web browser. Just bring a Google account.
What if I get stuck during practice?
Every exercise has hints! Plus, we'll review solutions together. You can also ask questions in the Zoom chat.
Can I use this for my research?
Absolutely! That's the whole point. We'll show you how to apply these skills to your own datasets.
What if I can't attend live?
All materials are available here anytime! The notebooks are self-paced. We'll also record the session.
Is there follow-up support?
Yes! We offer office hours, a Slack channel, and will schedule advanced workshops based on interest.
